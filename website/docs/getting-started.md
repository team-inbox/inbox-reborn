# Getting started

## 1. Install and reload Gmail

Install from the [Chrome Web Store](https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/inbox-reborn-theme-for-gm/bkphmihkdbdgedlaflnkhnmnmibffomf)
or [Firefox Add-ons](https://addons.mozilla.org/firefox/addon/inbox-reborn-theme-gmail/),
then reload any open Gmail tab. The extension runs only on `mail.google.com`.

Pin the extension's icon to your browser's toolbar (in Chrome: the puzzle-piece button, then
the pin next to Inbox Reborn). Clicking the icon opens the [settings popup](/settings).

## 2. Leave Stock Inbox on

New installs come up with **Stock Inbox** on. That one switch sets everything to Inbox's
own behavior: bundles (Purchases, Travel and Finance included), single-email bundles,
bundles in their own date group, avatars, no dates on the rows, and AI Inbox and All Mail
hidden from the left menu.

<img src="/inbox-reborn/img/options-light.png" alt="The settings popup with Stock Inbox on" class="narrow" />

Change any of those settings later and Stock Inbox turns itself off, keeping your change.
Turn it back on to go back to Inbox's values. Dark mode, Reminders and Gravatar photos are
yours either way.

## 3. Two Gmail settings worth changing

Inbox Reborn works with Gmail as it is, but Inbox never had category tabs, and bundles only
form inside the inbox itself. In Gmail, open **Settings (gear) → See all settings**:

1. **Inbox → Categories: leave only Primary ticked.** With the tabs off, all your inbox mail
   is in one list, where the extension can bundle it.
2. **Labels → Categories: click _show_ next to Social, Promotions and Forums** (and Updates if
   you want an Updates bundle). This makes Gmail put the category's chip on each inbox row,
   and the chip is what the extension bundles by. See
   [Gmail's categories](/bundles/gmail-categories) for why Updates is a judgment call.

![Gmail's Labels settings, where each category is set to show or hide in the message list](/img/gmail-settings-labels-categories.png)

Other settings that bring Gmail closer to Inbox's look:

| Gmail setting                          | Choose                                             |
| -------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Inbox → Inbox type                     | **Default** (or Starred first)                     |
| Inbox → Importance markers             | **No markers**                                     |
| General → Maximum page size            | **100** conversations per page                     |
| General → Personal level indicators    | **No indicators**                                  |
| Advanced → Multiple Inboxes            | **Disable**                                        |
| Advanced → Preview Pane                | **Disable**                                        |

## 4. What happens to your mail on the first day

**Nothing moves.** The extension draws Gmail differently; it does not archive, label or move
anything on its own. On day one:

- **The seven categories bundle.** Travel, Purchases, Finance, Social, Updates, Forums and
  Promotions sit in **Bundled in the inbox**, and the inbox mail Gmail has sorted into them
  collapses into bundles. The mail is still in your inbox; it is drawn as one row per bundle.
- **All of your own labels start in Unbundled.** Their emails show one by one, as in Gmail.
  To bundle a label, drag it up into Bundled in the inbox. The first drag up asks before it
  brings the label's archived mail into the inbox, so you can also say Cancel and let the
  bundle fill with new mail only.
- **Your phone and other devices see Gmail as usual.** Bundles are a way of drawing the
  inbox in this browser; they aren't labels or folders, and they don't exist on your phone.

The one way the extension moves mail is the drag between the two sections, described in
[Bundled in the inbox and Unbundled](/bundles/bundled-and-unbundled). Dragging a label down
archives its inbox mail (so the rows leave the inbox, as the bundle did) and remembers which
conversations went; dragging it back up asks, then brings exactly those back.

## 5. Dark mode

Turn on **Dark mode** in the settings popup. It applies instantly, in every Gmail tab, and
works whatever Gmail theme you have.
