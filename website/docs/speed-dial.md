# Speed dial

Hover the red **+** compose button at the bottom right. It fans out, as Inbox's did:

![The compose button fanned out into the reminder button and three recent contacts](/img/speed-dial.png)

- The **blue hand** starts a reminder: a new email to yourself, which the inbox then shows as
  a reminder (see [Reminders](/the-inbox#reminders)).
- The **three faces** are the people you have written to most recently. Hover one for their
  name and address; click it to start an email to them, already addressed.

## Where the contacts come from

The speed dial learns from your **sent mail**. Each time Gmail loads, the extension asks Gmail
for your newest sent conversations and takes the recipients from them, newest first. Sending
an email also moves its recipients to the top straight away. There is nothing to set up, and
it works the same in every browser you use the extension in.

The list stays in your browser's extension storage; see [Privacy](/privacy).
