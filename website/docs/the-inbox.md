# The inbox

## Date groups

The inbox is grouped the way Inbox grouped it: **Today**, **Yesterday**, **This week**,
**This month**, then one group per month, then one per year. Snoozed mail that has come back
and pinned mail sit in their groups by date, as everything else does.

With Stock Inbox on, rows carry no date or time; the headings carry the dates, as in Inbox.
Turn **Dates on emails** on under Customize → Email list in the [settings](/settings) to get
Gmail's date column back.

### Sweep

Every date heading has a ✓ at its right. Clicking it **marks the whole group done**: every
email in the group is archived, except pinned (starred) ones, which stay. Gmail shows its
usual _Undo_ afterwards, so a sweep is never final until you let the Undo go.

A bundle has a ✓ of its own when you hover it, which sweeps just that bundle.

## Hover actions

Move the mouse over an email and Inbox's actions appear at the right of the row, in Inbox's
order:

![An email row with Pin, Snooze, Trash, Done and the more menu showing](/img/row-hover.png)

| Action     | What it does                                                                                      |
| ---------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Pin**    | Stars the email. Pinned mail never joins a bundle and never gets swept.                           |
| **Snooze** | Opens Gmail's snooze picker. The email leaves the inbox and comes back at the time you choose.    |
| **Trash**  | Moves the email to Trash.                                                                         |
| **Done**   | Archives the email (Gmail's _Archive_). It leaves the inbox and stays in All Mail and under Done. |
| **⋮**      | Gmail's own menu for the row: mark as read or unread, labels, mute, and the rest.                 |

The same actions are on the toolbar when you open an email, and Gmail's keyboard shortcuts
work as they always did (`e` archives, `s` stars, `b` snoozes, `#` trashes).

## Pinned switch

The pin switch in the header, next to the search box, is Inbox's pin toggle. Turn it on and
the inbox shows only your **pinned (starred) mail and reminders**; turn it off for everything.
The left menu's title reads _Pinned_ while it's on.

![The inbox with the Pinned switch on, showing only starred mail and a reminder](/img/pinned.png)

## Inbox, Snoozed and Done

The top of the left menu is Inbox's: **Inbox**, **Snoozed** and **Done**. Done is Gmail's
archive (All Mail without the inbox), so a swept group or an archived email is one click away.

## Reminders

Inbox had reminders; Gmail doesn't. The extension shows **emails you send to yourself** as
reminders: a blue hand icon instead of an avatar, and the subject as the reminder text.
Write one with the reminder button on the [speed dial](/speed-dial), or send yourself any
email.

The **Reminders** setting chooses which of your emails to yourself count:

- **All** — every email from you to you
- **"Reminder" only** — only those whose subject is _Reminder_ (the default)
- **Off** — none; they show as ordinary emails

## Avatars

Each row starts with the sender's picture: a photo Gmail has shown you for that person, a
company's published logo, the sender's site icon, or, when none is found, a colored circle
with their initial. Turn **Gravatar photos** on in the settings to also look senders up on
Gravatar (off by default, because it shares a hashed copy of each address with Gravatar; see
[Privacy](/privacy)). **Avatars** under Customize → Email list turns the whole column off.

## Calendar events

An invitation shows on a small card with the event's date and time, and **Yes / Maybe / No**
buttons that answer it from the inbox.

## Dark mode

**Dark mode** in the settings popup switches the whole of Gmail to the extension's dark
theme, instantly, without touching Gmail's own theme setting. It covers the inbox, the open
email, the left menu, the settings popup, and the dialogs the extension draws.

![Gmail in Inbox Reborn's dark mode](/img/inbox-dark.png)

## Speed dial

Hover the red compose button for the reminder button and your three most recent contacts.
See [Speed dial](/speed-dial).
