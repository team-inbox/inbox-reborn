# Inbox Reborn manual

Inbox Reborn is a browser extension that brings the look and the habits of Google's
discontinued **Inbox** back to Gmail: a calm list grouped by date, bundles that collapse
your newsletters, receipts and notifications into single rows, Pin and Snooze and Done on
every email, a left menu split into **Bundled in the inbox** and **Unbundled**, and a dark
mode that works with any Gmail theme.

![Gmail with Inbox Reborn: date groups, bundles, avatars and Inbox's left menu](/img/inbox-light.png)

It is a layer on top of Gmail. Your mail stays in Gmail, Gmail's own features keep working,
and if you turn the extension off, Gmail is exactly as you left it.

## Install

- **Chrome, Edge, Brave and other Chromium browsers:**
  [Chrome Web Store](https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/inbox-reborn-theme-for-gm/bkphmihkdbdgedlaflnkhnmnmibffomf)
- **Firefox:** [Firefox Add-ons](https://addons.mozilla.org/firefox/addon/inbox-reborn-theme-gmail/)

After installing, reload Gmail. New installs start with **Stock Inbox** on, which sets
everything the way Inbox had it. See [Getting started](/getting-started) for the two Gmail
settings worth changing on day one.

## A one-minute tour

1. **Date groups.** Today, Yesterday, This week, then the months. Each heading has a ✓ on the
   right: **Sweep**, which marks the whole group done. Pinned emails stay.
2. **Bundles.** Rows like _Updates (12)_ or _Receipts (3)_ are bundles. They list who wrote,
   and clicking one opens the mail inside. Hover a bundle for its own ✓.
3. **Hover an email** for Inbox's actions: **Pin**, **Snooze**, **Trash**, **Done**, and ⋮ for
   the rest of Gmail's menu.
4. **The left menu.** Inbox, Snoozed and Done at the top. Then **Bundled in the inbox**
   (Travel, Purchases, Finance, Social, Updates, Forums, Promotions, and the labels you've
   dragged up) and **Unbundled** (everything else). Drag a label between the two to change
   how its mail shows.
5. **The red compose button.** Hover it for the speed dial: a reminder button and your three
   most recent contacts.
6. **The extension's icon** in the browser toolbar opens the settings popup.

:::tip Nothing happens to your mail until you ask
Installing the extension, and turning its switches on and off, changes only how Gmail is
drawn. The one thing that moves mail is **dragging a label or category between Bundled in the
inbox and Unbundled**, and a drag that brings mail into the inbox always asks first. See
[what moves when you drag](/bundles/bundled-and-unbundled#what-moves-when-you-drag).
:::
