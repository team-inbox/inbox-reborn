# Chrome Web Store listing - Inbox Reborn 2.5

## Summary (132 characters max)

Google Inbox, back in Gmail: bundles, sweep, pins, reminders, speed dial and dark mode. One switch sets it all up.

## Description

Inbox Reborn brings Google Inbox back to Gmail. Turn on Stock Inbox and your Gmail reads and works the way Inbox did; customize anything after.

BUNDLES, LIKE INBOX'S
• Travel, Purchases, Finance, Social, Updates, Forums and Promotions bundle on their own, in Inbox's colors and order, with Gmail's own sorting behind them.
• Bundle any label: the left menu is split into "Bundled in the inbox" and "Unbundled". Drag a label up and its mail collapses into a bundle; drag it down and it shows as rows again. Your mail moves with it.
• Each email sits in one bundle, and a travel label of yours (Cruise, Trips) counts as Travel.
• Fold either section of the menu up with the arrow beside its title.

THE INBOX, THE INBOX WAY
• Mail grouped by date: Today, Yesterday, This week, This month…
• Sweep: one click on a date heading marks the whole group done. Pinned mail stays. Gmail's Undo brings it back.
• Pins instead of stars, with a Pinned switch in the header that shows only what you pinned.
• Reminders: an email to yourself shows as a reminder, with Inbox's icon.
• Hover actions on every row: Pin, Snooze, Trash, Done, and the rest of Gmail's menu.
• Sender pictures on the avatars where there is one; a colored initial otherwise.
• Calendar invitations as a small card with inline responses.

SPEED DIAL
Hover the red Compose button for the people you write to most, and start an email to one of them in a click.

DARK MODE
A built-in dark theme in Inbox's own colors, for the inbox, conversations, compose and settings. It works with any Gmail theme.

STOCK INBOX
One switch, on for new installs, that sets everything to the way Inbox had it: bundles, avatars, the clean list and Inbox's left menu. Change one thing and the switch remembers your change.

PRIVACY
Everything runs in your browser. The extension asks Gmail, with your own session, which inbox mail is Travel, Purchases and Finance, and nothing leaves your machine unless you turn on Gravatar photos. Full policy: https://github.com/team-inbox/inbox-reborn/blob/master/PRIVACY.md
